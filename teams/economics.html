Research Team

Economics & Labor

The Economics & Labor team applies rigorous quantitative and qualitative analysis to the policy challenges shaping American livelihoods — from housing affordability crises and wage stagnation to trade imbalances and financial regulation. We translate complex economic evidence into clear legislative recommendations.

Focus Areas

  • Housing affordability and federal homelessness policy
  • Labor market disparities and wage policy
  • Trade policy and domestic manufacturing
  • Financial regulation and consumer protection
  • Gig economy classification and worker benefits
  • Child poverty and safety net reform
  • Immigration economics and labor supply
