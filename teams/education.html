Research Team

Education Policy

The Education Policy team investigates the structural forces shaping learning outcomes — from school climate and disciplinary disparities to pandemic recovery and global access. We produce briefs for school districts, state boards of education, federal agencies, and international organizations.

Focus Areas

  • School climate and student well-being interventions
  • School-to-prison pipeline and disciplinary reform
  • Pandemic learning loss and equity-centered recovery
  • Global education access and international frameworks
  • School resource officer policy and safety
  • Teacher pipeline and workforce development
  • Higher education affordability and access
