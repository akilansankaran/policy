Technology Policy

The Technology Policy studies the manner in which significant technologies, particularly from the standpoint of frontier models being deployed in healthcare settings, intersect with regulatory frameworks pertaining to privacy. We work to ensure that legislation keeps pace with innovation while protecting the public interest.

Focus Areas

  • AI governance and algorithmic accountability
  • Genomic data privacy
  • Energetic transitions
  • Regulatory frameworks pertaining to autonomy
  • Efforts for de-identification of data post-hoc
  • Digital equity
