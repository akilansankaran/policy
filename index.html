About the Policy Program

The Policy Program is Harvard IOP's undergraduate-run think tank, giving students the opportunity to develop skills in policy research and writing while effecting real-world change. Research from the Policy Program has helped develop new legislation, build community programs, and analyze pressing issues facing communities and states across the country. In the past, we have worked with state, federal, and international commissioners.

Students meet weekly with their research teams to discuss their work, learn best practices in policy writing, and hear from distinguished guest speakers. At semester's end, students present their completed briefs to commissioners and community stakeholders. No prior policy research experience is required — our goal is to spark genuine interest in public policy and for every student to learn something new.
