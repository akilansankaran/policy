Directory

People

The Policy Program is led by student co-chairs and supported by IOP faculty advisors and staff. Research teams are composed of undergraduate students who apply each semester.

Student Leadership

Program Co-Chairs

Co-Chair

Matthew Tobin

Class of 2027 · Winthrop House · Social Studies & Economics

Matt manages commissioner relationships, oversees research quality, and represents the program within IOP. He previously worked with U.S. Senate Majority Leader Chuck Schumer and the States United Democracy Center. He is also Executive Director of the Harvard Undergraduate Legal Committee and a Crimson columnist.

Co-Chair

Alisa Lee

Class of 2028 · Quincy House · Neuroscience & Government

Alisa oversees team operations, recruits research members, and coordinates deliverable timelines. She brings an interdisciplinary perspective rooted in neuroscience and public policy. Active in Harvard Undergraduate Consulting on Business and the Environment and Harvard Club Quadball.

Research Teams

Team Directors

Each research team is led by a student director who manages weekly meetings, coordinates with commissioners, and guides the brief-writing process.

Health Policy Director Oversees health & public health research team
Technology Policy Director Oversees tech regulation & energy research team
Education Policy Director Oversees K–12, higher ed, & global education team
Economics & Labor Director Oversees labor, housing, & trade research team
Director of Outreach Commissioner relations & brief dissemination
Director of Communications Publications, web presence, & IOP coordination

Get Involved

Join the Program

Harvard undergraduates are encouraged to apply each semester. Research members attend weekly team meetings, contribute to brief-writing, and interact directly with policy commissioners. No prior policy experience is required — only genuine curiosity and commitment.
